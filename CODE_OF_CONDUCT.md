# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a welcoming, respectful, and
constructive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good intent and give the benefit of the doubt.
- Be kind, patient, and welcoming to newcomers.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and apologize to those affected.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, including in issues and reviews.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, intimidation, or public and private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior by contacting the maintainer privately. All
reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
